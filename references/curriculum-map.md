# Curriculum map (Zangak textbooks, ՀՀ ԿԳՄՍ նախարարություն)

Authors of the main series: Հովհաննես Զաքարյան, Յուրի Ավետիսյան, Գայանե Մկրտչյան, Լիլիթ Գրիգորյան. Publisher «Զանգակ». Alternative series: Փառանձեմ Մեյթիխանյան et al. («Աստղիկ Գրատուն»), editors Վիկտոր Կատվալյան, Փառանձեմ Մեյթիխանյան; grade 10 also Գոհար Մուրադյան.

## Grade 7 — Morphology I (176 pp.)
Grammar & its sections; word form, base, morphological suffix. Parts of speech (10). Noun: meaning/structure, common vs proper, number, plural formation, collective/uncountable/plural-only, definite vs indefinite, cases, declension types. Adjective: meaning/structure, quality vs relation, degrees, uses. Numeral: types, spelling, notation, agreement. Pronoun: types (personal, demonstrative, reciprocal, interrogative, relative, definite, indefinite, negative), articles. Verb: meaning, affixes, voice, non-finite forms, finite forms and conjugation, mood and tense, person and number, tense-forms and extra uses, irregular and defective verbs, complex verbs.

## Grade 8 — Morphology II and simple-sentence syntax (176 pp.)
Adverb; postposition (կապ); conjunction; modal word (վերաբերական); interjection; morphological analysis. Syntax: what syntax is; sentence; sentence members (subject, predicate, attribute, apposition, complement, adverbial); non-members; word combinations; participial phrases; word order; logical stress; communicative types; one-member sentences; sentence diagrams.

## Grade 9 — Complex sentences, quoted speech, speech genres (176 pp.)
Coordinate and subordinate clauses; ellipsis; correlation; word order in complex sentences; multi-component sentences; direct and indirect speech; dialogue in different styles; interview; monologue; public speaking; essay and its varieties (including analytical essay).

## Grade 10 — Language history, phonetics/orthography, lexicology, word formation, text (180 pp.)
From the history of Armenian; spelling and orthoepy of vowels, յ, diphthongs, և, consonants, doubled consonants; sound change (stress shift); words and meaning; synonyms/antonyms/homonyms/paronyms; layers of vocabulary; word structure and compounds; abbreviation; dictionaries; text; business writing (application, autobiography, explanatory note). Appendix: general tasks.

## Using the map
- Lesson plan for grade N: take the topics above, add vocabulary and exercises matching the topics' difficulty; each textbook lesson typically has: goal ("Կծանոթանաք… Կպարզեք… Կիմանաք…"), glossary (Եզրույթներ), theory, exercises (Գիտելիքի կիրառում / խորացում / ամրակայում), research tasks (Քննարկում), vocabulary box (Բառապաշար), and self-assessment (Ինքնագնահատում: "Արդեն գիտեմ… Արդեն կարող եմ…").
- Do not give a grade 7 student grade 9+ syntax terms without explanation.
